A sci-fi book set on Novella, a dangerous blue planet.

The story follows multiple perspectives of different groups on the planet getting in each other's way, usually leading to deadly conflict.

Groups include: a dangerous politician and his advisors, small group of rebels, a legendary animal hunter, drug dealers, drug buyers, and a group of grunts.

Each group has their own goals: power, survival, morality, money, and revenge.

The main story focus is about a brother in a high position of power trying to hunt his sister down after a failed mission resulting in a lot of civilian casualties. This leads to the groups getting in the way of each other. The overall focus is that none of the groups are good people.
